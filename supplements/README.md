# Supplements

> **Safety note:** supplements can interact with each other and with
> medicines, and some are harmful in high doses. Nothing here is a
> recommendation to take anything. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md) and
> talk to a doctor or pharmacist first.

**Status:** Planned. No content yet.

## Scope

- How to evaluate a supplement: what the evidence says, and how to tell a
  strong source from marketing.
- How to read a Supplement Facts label: dose, form, and upper limits.
- Timing and interactions that affect absorption.
- Lessons on keeping a routine simple enough to follow.

Not in scope: personal stacks or personal dosages.
