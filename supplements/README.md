# Supplements

> **Safety note:** supplements can interact with medicines, lab tests and each
> other (confirm with a pharmacist or doctor), and some are harmful in high
> amounts. This module recommends no
> supplement, dose, timing or combination. Read
> [DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md) and talk to a doctor, pharmacist or
> registered dietitian before starting anything.

**Status:** Draft. Sources checked 2026-10-02.

**Who it is for:** anyone deciding whether a supplement is worth taking, or
trying to make sense of a label or a health claim, who wants to judge it for
themselves before asking a professional.

## Start here

1. Quickest route: paste [ai-context.md](ai-context.md) into any AI
   assistant and use its **guided session** prompt. It walks you through the
   five checks and ends with a short report for a pharmacist to confirm.
   Or read the [guide](guide.md) and do the checks yourself.
2. Use the free tools listed there: NIH fact sheets, the Dietary Supplement
   Label Database, and your pharmacist.
3. Skim the [lessons](lessons.md) on keeping a routine simple and honest.
