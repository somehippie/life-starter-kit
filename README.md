# Life Starter Kit

Practical starter kits for parts of everyday life: what to buy, in what order,
what it costs, and what tends to go wrong. Each kit is written from real
experience, generalized so anyone can use it, and checked against dated
sources.

**Not professional advice.** Read [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md) before acting
on anything here, especially the health and safety modules.

## How to use it

1. Pick a module below and open its README. It says who the kit is for and where to start.
2. Follow `guide.md`: ordered steps, with budget, mid and upgrade tiers.
3. Read `lessons.md` for what tends to work and what does not.
4. Want it tailored to you? Paste the module's `ai-context.md` into any AI assistant and use the prompts at the end of it.
5. Check `sources.md` before buying. Prices and models change; every fact there has a "last verified" date.
