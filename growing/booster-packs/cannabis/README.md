# Growing: cannabis

> **Legal notice. Read this first.**
>
> 1. **California adults 21 and over only.** This Booster Pack is written
>    for California. Growing cannabis may be illegal where you live.
>    **Growing cannabis at home for personal use remains illegal under US
>    federal law.** Since April 2026 some marijuana is in Schedule III, but
>    only FDA-approved products and marijuana under a state medical license.
>    Home-grown cannabis for adult use is in neither group, so it stays in
>    Schedule I. Federal law treats growing as manufacturing, which is
>    illegal without federal registration in any schedule.
> 2. **Six living plants at most, per private residence.** The limit is per
>    home, at one time, however many people live there.
> 3. **Locked and out of sight.** Plants must be kept at the residence or on
>    its grounds, in a locked space, and not visible by normal unaided vision
>    from a public place.
> 4. **Local rules apply too.** Cities and counties can add their own rules,
>    such as permits or other limits, and can ban outdoor growing. They
>    cannot fully ban growing inside a private residence, or inside an
>    enclosed, secure structure on its grounds. Check your city's and
>    county's rules.
> 5. **No sales.** Personal, noncommercial use only. Giving cannabis to
>    another adult is allowed only without any payment of any kind, and
>    possessing cannabis for sale is a crime under state law.
>
> Not legal advice. Laws change; the sources and the date they were checked
> are in [sources.md](sources.md). Read [DISCLAIMER.md](../../../DISCLAIMER.md).
