## Legal notice (California)

| Claim | Value | Source | Last verified | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lawful for adults 21 and over to grow | "persons 21 years of age or older" may "possess, plant, cultivate, harvest, dry, or process not more than six living cannabis plants" | [California Health and Safety Code 11362.1(a)(3)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=HSC&sectionNum=11362.1) | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| Six plants per residence, at one time | "Not more than six living plants ... within a single private residence, or upon the grounds of that private residence, at one time" | [HSC 11362.2(a)(3)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=HSC&sectionNum=11362.2) | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| However many people live there | The limit is set per residence, not per person | HSC 11362.2(a)(3) | 2026-10-04 | inferred from HSC 11362.2(a)(3) wording |
| Locked space, not visible from a public place | Kept within the residence or on its grounds, "in a locked space, and are not visible by normal unaided vision from a public place" | HSC 11362.2(a)(2) | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| Local rules apply | Growing must follow local ordinances; cities and counties "may enact and enforce reasonable regulations" | HSC 11362.2(a)(1), (b)(1) | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| Permits as an example of local rules | Not named in the statute; covered by "reasonable regulations" | HSC 11362.2(b)(1) | 2026-10-04 | inferred from HSC 11362.2(b)(1) wording |
| Locals can ban outdoor growing | May "completely prohibit" growing "outdoors upon the grounds of a private residence" | HSC 11362.2(b)(3) | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| Locals cannot fully ban indoor growing | "shall not completely prohibit" growing inside a private residence, or inside an accessory structure on its grounds "that is fully enclosed and secure" | HSC 11362.2(b)(2) | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| Giving away only without payment | Adults may give away up to 28.5 grams to adults 21 and over "without any compensation whatsoever" | HSC 11362.1(a)(1) | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| Possessing for sale is a crime | "Every person who possesses for sale any cannabis, except as otherwise provided by law, shall be punished" | [HSC 11359](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=HSC&sectionNum=11359) | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| Current federal status: marijuana in Schedule I | "Marihuana" is listed in Schedule I, which covers it "unless listed in another schedule" | [21 CFR 1308.11(d)(23)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1308/section-1308.11), eCFR text current as of 2026-10-01 | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| Schedule III covers only two groups | Since 2026-04-28, marijuana "in a U.S. Food and Drug Administration approved product or subject to a state medical marijuana license" is in Schedule III. A broader rescheduling is still before the DEA. | [21 CFR 1308.13(g)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-II/part-1308/section-1308.13), [DEA final rule 2026-08176, 2026-04-28](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/28/2026-08176/schedules-of-controlled-substances-rescheduling-of-food-and-drug-administration-approved-products) | 2026-10-04 | read at source |
| Home growing for adult use stays in Schedule I | It is neither an FDA-approved product nor under a state medical license | 21 CFR 1308.11(d), 1308.13(g) | 2026-10-04 | inferred from 21 CFR 1308.11 and 1308.13 wording |
| Growing is manufacturing, illegal without registration in any schedule | "production" includes "planting, cultivation, growing, or harvesting"; "manufacture" means "the production ... of a drug or other substance"; it is unlawful "to manufacture ... a controlled substance" "except as authorized" | [21 USC 802(15), (22)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/21/802), [21 USC 841(a)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/21/841) (Cornell LII copy of the US Code) | 2026-10-04 | inferred from 21 USC 802 and 841 wording |
