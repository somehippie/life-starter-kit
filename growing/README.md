# Growing

Growing things at home, from a first houseplant toward a garden that feeds
you. Separate from [Hobbies](../hobbies/): the long-term direction is
homesteading.

**Status:** Planned. Scope only; no guide yet.

**Who it is for:** someone who wants to keep plants alive and thriving,
starting with forgiving ones.

**Reader types:**

- **Never kept a plant alive:** wants one or two forgiving plants and the
  few habits that keep them healthy.
- **Has a few plants, ready for more:** wants to know what to add next and
  how to care for each.
