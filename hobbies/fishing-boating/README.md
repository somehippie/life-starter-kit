# Fishing and boating

> **Safety note:** water is dangerous. Wear a life jacket, check the weather,
> and follow local boating and fishing rules, including licences. Read
> [DISCLAIMER.md](../../DISCLAIMER.md).

**Status:** Planned. Scope only; no guide yet.

**Who it is for:** someone who wants to start fishing or get out on a boat,
and wants to know what to buy and what to learn first.

**Reader types:**

- **Curious but unsure:** wants to try it without buying much.
- **Ready to buy gear:** wants a short list of what to buy first and why.

## Scope

Content will come from the owner's own experience, written up from exported
chat notes and checked against dated sources.
