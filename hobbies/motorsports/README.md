# Motorsports

> **Safety note:** riding and driving fast can kill or injure you and others.
> Wear proper protective gear, get trained, and follow local laws and track
> rules. Read [DISCLAIMER.md](../../DISCLAIMER.md).

**Status:** Planned. Scope only; no guide yet.

**Who it is for:** anyone drawn to riding or driving for the feeling of it,
who wants to see the whole landscape before choosing where to start.

**Reader types:**

- **Curious, not sure where to start:** wants to see what exists and what
  each kind of riding or driving feels like before spending anything.
- **Ready to pick a discipline:** knows the feeling they are after and wants
  the shortest route to a first machine, gear and training.
- **Already riding or driving:** wants to know what else is out there and
  what carries over.

## Scope

The theme is optical flow, the sense of motion as the world streams past
you, and the flow state that comes with it.

The starter kit will be a map of what exists, so a reader can find the kind
that fits them:

- Track
- Street
- Dirtbike
- ADV (adventure riding)
- Grom stunting
- Scooters and automatics
- Gas vs. electric, across all of the above
- E-scooters
- Drift cars
- Big-wheel drift bikes

Trikes get a one-line mention. Later Booster Packs may cover powered
paragliders and hydrofoils.
