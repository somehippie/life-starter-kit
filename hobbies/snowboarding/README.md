# Snowboarding

> **Safety note:** snowboarding carries a real risk of injury. Wear a helmet,
> take a lesson, and follow the mountain's rules. Read
> [DISCLAIMER.md](../../DISCLAIMER.md).

**Status:** Planned. Scope only; no guide yet.

**Who it is for:** someone who wants to learn to snowboard, or to ride with
more style, without wasting money on the wrong gear.

**Reader types:**

- **Never been, or rented a few times:** wants to know whether to rent or
  buy, and how to get through the first days.
- **Ready to buy gear:** wants a short list of what to buy first and why.

## Scope

The owner's own style, perspective and gear recommendations, written up
from interviews and checked against dated sources.
