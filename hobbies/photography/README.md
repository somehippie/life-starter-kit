# Photography

**Status:** Planned. Scope only; no guide yet.

**Who it is for:** someone who wants to take better photos, with the phone
they already have or with their first dedicated camera.

**Reader types:**

- **Shooting on a phone, curious about more:** wants to know what a camera
  would add, and what to learn first.
- **Ready to buy a camera:** wants a short list of what to buy first and why.

## Scope

The owner's own style, perspective and gear recommendations, written up
from interviews and checked against dated sources.

Photos added to this module will have all metadata, especially GPS
location, stripped before they are committed (see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#content-rules)).
