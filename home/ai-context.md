# Home: AI context

## Purpose

Helps someone improve their home by choosing the right mix of products and
services without overspending. Covers two improvements so far: fragrance-free
soap in every dispenser, and hot tub upkeep on a fixed schedule. Not
professional advice; manuals and local professionals take precedence.

## Key facts

Prices checked 2026-10-02; they change.

- Soap dispenser options: keep existing dispensers and change only the soap
  (budget); Secura 17 oz Automatic, ~$24-29, 4 × AA batteries (mid);
  simplehuman 9 oz Liquid Sensor Pump, $70-80, rechargeable, about 4 months
  per charge, 2-year warranty (upgrade).
- Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser is listed by its maker as accepted by the
  National Eczema Association (NEA). Confirm current status on the NEA site.
- "Fragrance-free" is the label to look for. "Unscented" can contain masking
  fragrance.
- Never top off a part-full soap dispenser: empty, wash, dry, refill.
- Hot tub (Caldera guidance): skimmer basket weekly, filter rinse monthly,
  filter replacement every 3 years, drain and refill every 3-4 months on
  chlorine or bromine.
- FROG @ease sanitizer: SmartChlor cartridge about every 3-4 weeks (400 gal
  tub), mineral cartridge every 4 months, non-chlorine shock monthly or at each
  SmartChlor change. Balance: pH 7.2-7.8, alkalinity 80-120 ppm, hardness
  150-250 ppm. Adjust alkalinity first; one chemical at a time; wait 6 hours.
- FROG @ease needs its own test strips; standard strips misread its chlorine
  reserve. A basic 3-way kit (Taylor K-1000) does not measure alkalinity.

## Decision rules

- If the goal is gentler soap, change the soap before changing the dispenser.
- If buying new dispensers for several sinks, buy one first and trial it at
  the busiest sink for a few weeks.
- If the trial dispenser struggles with the chosen soap, switch dispenser type
  before buying more.
- If you prefer not to buy batteries, choose the rechargeable option; if you
  want fewer refills and a lower price, choose the larger battery model.
- If you install equipment that needs upkeep, record the install date and set
  recurring calendar events for every task on day one.
- If your hot tub manual and this kit disagree, follow the manual.

## Common mistakes

- Buying a dispenser for every sink before testing one with your soap.
- Choosing "unscented" when "fragrance-free" was needed.
- Topping off soap dispensers instead of emptying and washing them.
- Using a test kit that does not match the sanitizer system, then
  "correcting" water that was fine.
- Adjusting pH before alkalinity, or adding several chemicals at once.
